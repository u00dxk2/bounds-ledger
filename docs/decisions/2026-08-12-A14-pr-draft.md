## Why that specific form

The repo's own convention, from its README legend:

> Bounds for which the level of available verification is currently at minimal levels will be
> marked with an asterisk in the table below.

3b's summary-table row carries the asterisk: `| 1.77898 (1.77898884*) | 1.83333 |`. The
improvement line announcing the same bound carries neither the asterisk nor the `(unverified)`
tag that comparable lines carry.

**The cleanest evidence is the adjacent constant.** 3a is in the same situation — asterisked row
— and both of its improvement lines put the asterisk on the value:

```
- [3a] **improved lower bound (limit value):** $C_{3a} \geq 1.187326127925948*$ …
- [3a] **improved lower bound (limit value):** $C_{3a} \geq 1.19102809*$ …
- [3b] **improved lower bound:**             $C_{3b} \geq 1.77898884$   ← no asterisk
```

So the proposed fix copies the repo's own existing style from three lines above, rather than
inventing one.
