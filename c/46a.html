Outside the source table: a claim in OpenAI's math release

  • Family 079, “Local smoothing for the Euclidean wave equation”, claims that the extension operator of the sphere is bounded from L^a of the sphere to L^b of the whole space in every dimension n ≥ 2, whenever a > 1, b > 2n/(n−1) and b > (n+1)/(n−1)·a′.

    Its Lean statement, LocalSmoothing.lean:

    theorem spherical_restriction : ∀ n : ℕ, 2 ≤ n → ∀ a : ℝ≥0∞, 1 < a → ∀ b : ℝ, 2 * (n : ℝ) / (n - 1) < b → ((n : ℝ) + 1) / ((n : ℝ) - 1) * conjugateExponent a < b → ∃ C : ℝ, 0 < C ∧ ∀ g : Space n → ℂ, MemLp g a (sphereMeasure n) → MemLp (sphericalExtension g) (ENNReal.ofReal b) ∧ eLpNorm (sphericalExtension g) (ENNReal.ofReal b) volume ≤ ENNReal.ofReal C * eLpNorm g a (sphereMeasure n)

    Take n = 3 and a = ∞, so a′ = 1: the conditions become b > 3 and b > 2, and the statement gives the bound this constant defines, from L^∞ of the 2-sphere to L^b of 3-space, for every b > 3. That would make C₄₆ at most 3, equal to its listed lower bound. Lean's sphere measure and its exp(+i x·ω) convention differ from the definition's surface measure and Fourier transform only by constants and a reflection, which change no L^∞ or L^b bound. The release calls its manuscripts raw research data at different stages of verification (README) and says it published this one as the model's output (history.md, 26 October 2026); neither document reports a human check of this proof.
