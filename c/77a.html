Outside the source table: a claim in OpenAI's math release

  • Family 079, “Local smoothing for the Euclidean wave equation”, claims that the Bochner–Riesz means of order δ are bounded on L^p in every dimension n ≥ 2, for every 1 ≤ p ≤ ∞ and δ > max(n·|1/p − 1/2| − 1/2, 0), uniformly in the radius.

    Its Lean statement, LocalSmoothing.lean:

    theorem bochner_riesz : ∀ n : ℕ, 2 ≤ n → ∀ p : ℝ≥0∞, 1 ≤ p → ∀ δ : ℝ, max ((n : ℝ) * |(p⁻¹).toReal - 1 / 2| - 1 / 2) 0 < δ → ∃ C : ℝ, 0 < C ∧ ∀ R : ℝ, 0 < R → ∀ f : SchwartzMap (Space n) ℂ, MemLp (bochnerRiesz δ R f) p ∧ eLpNorm (bochnerRiesz δ R f) p volume ≤ ENNReal.ofReal C * eLpNorm f p volume

    Take n = 3: the order condition is this constant's λ > λ₃,ₚ. The statement bounds the operator on Schwartz functions; for finite p that bound extends to all of Lᵖ by density. At p = 1 and p = ∞ the condition is λ > 1, where the Bochner–Riesz kernel is integrable (a classical fact, not part of the Lean statement), so those endpoints hold by convolution. The range in the definition is then established for every p, and Γ = 3 qualifies. That would make C₇₇ at most 3, equal to its listed lower bound. The statement is uniform in the radius R, so the 2π convention of the Fourier transform does not matter. The release calls its manuscripts raw research data at different stages of verification (README) and says it published this one as the model's output (history.md, 26 October 2026); neither document reports a human check of this proof.

    Status: a Lean statement of it is published with a proof by its authors. We read the statement and it matches this constant's definition; we have not compiled the proof, and we have not read the paper.
