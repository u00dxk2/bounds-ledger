Outside the source table: a claim in OpenAI's math release

  • Family 039, “Nagata’s conjecture and maximal Seshadri constants”, claims that for 10 or more very general points in the plane, every curve of degree d with multiplicity at least m_i at the i-th point has m_1 + … + m_r < d·√r.

    Its Lean statement, Nagata.lean:

    theorem nagata_conjecture : ∀ count : ℕ, 10 ≤ count → ∃ exceptional : ℕ → Set (OrderedDistinctPoints count), (∀ index, IsConfigurationZariskiClosed (exceptional index) ∧ exceptional index ≠ Set.univ) ∧ (∃ points : OrderedDistinctPoints count, ∀ index, points ∉ exceptional index) ∧ ∀ points : OrderedDistinctPoints count, (∀ index, points ∉ exceptional index) → ∀ curve : Workers.W10.EffectivePlaneCurve, ∀ multiplicities : Fin count → ℕ, (∀ index, multiplicities index ≤ W15.curveOrdinaryMultiplicity curve (points.val index)) → (∑ index, (multiplicities index : ℝ)) < (Workers.W10.curveDegree curve : ℝ) * Real.sqrt (count : ℝ)

    For 10 points the statement says every plane curve through at least one of the points has degree divided by its total multiplicity at the points above 1/√10, for points outside a countable union of proper closed sets (very general points). This constant is the infimum of that ratio, so it is at least 1/√10, and with its upper-bound row 1/√10 it is exactly 1/√10, against 117/370 in its last-listed lower-bound row. Taking the infimum is our step; the file states the inequality curve by curve.

    Status: a Lean statement of it is published with a proof by its authors. We read the statement and it matches this constant's definition; we have not compiled the proof, and we have not read the paper.
