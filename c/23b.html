| $1/2$ | [Pat2026] | For odd $n$ every off-diagonal entry of $G=A^TA$ is odd, so with $\lambda\_k$ the eigenvalues of $G$: $\sum(\lambda\_k-n)=0$ and $\sum(\lambda\_k-n)^2\ge n(n-1)$. The Bhatia–Davis inequality gives $(\lambda\_{\max}-n)(n-\lambda\_{\min})\ge n-1$, hence $\kappa(A)^2\ge 1+2\sqrt{n-1}/n$ and $\kappa(n)\ge 1+(2-\sqrt2)/\sqrt n$ for every odd $n\ge3$. So $\kappa(n)=1+f(n)/n^\alpha$ with subpolynomial $f$ forces $\alpha\le 1/2$. |Cited in this row: [Pat2026], whose entry in upstream's reference list links to https://github.com/vidigoat/optimization-records/tree/main/tao.value changed 2026-10-26
